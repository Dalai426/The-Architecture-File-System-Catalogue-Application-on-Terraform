# Cost Management and Terraform References

Official documentation:
https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cost-management/latest/userguide/budgets-managing-costs.html

Purpose:
Use AWS Budgets to define a monthly spending limit and configure email notifications when costs reach specified thresholds.

https://registry.terraform.io/providers/hashicorp/aws/latest/docs/resources/budgets_budget
